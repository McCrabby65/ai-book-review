# Chapter 45: I Will Know

We had a few hours before the priests' coaches were leaving. Vagid and I agreed that he should help his parents, and I would continue on to Nara's parents and help them. When we were done, we would help Orrin's parents.

When Vagid got to his parents' house, I could hear Ruta. "Rushing your poor crippled mother early in the morning."

"Mom, let's go," said Vagid.

Nara's parents would take longer since her family was larger. I knocked on the door, and it flew open. "Evan, look at my scar," said Pip, pointing proudly at his scar.

"I'm sure the girls are very impressed," I said.

I followed Pip into the house. I heard Arlo sigh. "Boring."

I found Isla and Rowan talking in hushed voices in the kitchen. The conversation stopped when I walked in. "I'm sorry to interrupt. Should I help Grandma or Grandpa?" I asked.

Rowan looked at me with open dislike. "No." Then he left the kitchen, pushing past me.

I watched him leave, then turned to Isla. "I'm sorry if I've caused Rowan any concern."

Isla sighed and said, "He has trouble when his routine is changed. He will calm down once he's in the coach."

"Is there anything that I can help you with?"

"Yes. Can you grab the bags on the floor there? I think Pip and Arlo have their bags packed and ready, but if you could ask them again, that would be very helpful."

I picked up the bags and walked back to the living room, where Pip and Arlo were bickering.

"Hey, Pip, Arlo, where are your bags?" I asked.

Arlo pointed to a bag by the door. "The dark one is mine." *Typical Goth.*

Pip pointed to a bag leaning against Arlo's bag. "That's mine."

"Great. I need both of you to help me get everyone to the chapel. It's a big job, but you both look strong enough for it," I said.

Arlo sighed again. *Is he taking lessons from Prue?*

"Sure, Evan," said Pip.

Right then, Rowan and Grandpa came downstairs. "EVAN, WHEN DID YOU GET HERE?" said Grandpa. Rowan still seemed to be upset with me, but there wasn't anything I could do about it.

I waved to Grandpa.

A second later, Isla and Grandma came down.

"I can take Grandma's bag if that will help," I offered.

"Thank you, Evan," said Isla, handing the bag over.

We filed out of the house, and Rowan locked it up, then shuttered the windows. The walk to the chapel wasn't far, but took over half an hour. On our way, Vagid passed us and said to me, "I'll get Rafe, Lida, and Katia."

"Thanks," I said.

We got all the bags loaded onto one of the coaches, and the family seated and ready to go. Vagid and Orrin's family showed up as I was finishing up with Nara's family.

"Evan!" called Katia. She was wrapped up in my cloak, with one mummified arm waving to me.

"Hi, Katia!" I said.

I helped Vagid and Rafe load the bags while Lida and Katia found seats.

Vagid and I were checking that all the family members were doing okay when I heard, "Trying to skip town?"

I turned to see Prue and Mara walking up. I reached out for Prue's hand and said, "You caught me."

Mara walked up to Vagid and said, "Hi, Vagid! It's good to see you again!"

"Hi, Mara. You look really good," said Vagid as his ears flamed red.

Prue came in close and we kissed. As we separated, I saw Mara watching, then she turned to Vagid, pulled him in, and kissed him. Very passionately. I looked at the coach that Vagid's parents were in. Ruta's mouth was hanging open, but Faren just smiled.

Mara let Vagid go, and he staggered a bit and said, "Eep." I put my hand on his shoulder and guided him to the coach where his parents were sitting. We climbed in and took our seats.

"You have a female friend? Who you kiss?" said Ruta. "And your poor mother had to find out like this?"

Faren chuckled. Vagid was still dazed and blushing furiously. *I feel like I should splash him with water before his head explodes.*

"She's lovely," said Ruta. "What's her name?"

*This is going to be a fun ride.*

"Mom, her name is Mara. She works at the Guild. We've been to dinner a few times," said Vagid. Ruta opened her mouth. Faren patted her hand, and she closed it again.

At midmorning, the coaches departed for Garnerby. It wasn't a long trip, just a few hours. Garnerby was still more scaffolding than town. New timber stood beside old walls with a brown line across them at shoulder height. Ruta went quiet when she saw it, one hand on her ribs. Pip didn't offer to show anyone his scar. We got all the families settled with the relief workers before dinner.

Rowan seemed to have calmed down over the coach ride.

After dinner, Vagid and I said our goodbyes and started through the forest. It was a hunter's moon, so we could make out the trails through the trees.

We weren't sure if Silas's watchers were still there or not, but we didn't want to risk it.

We made camp in the woods a half league from Garnerby. We didn't make a fire and just ate the jerky and cheese we had in our packs. I took first watch, which was uneventful. As my watch ended, I woke Vagid, then drifted off to sleep.

In the morning, we had a breakfast of more jerky and cheese, then broke camp and continued on to the next town to catch the coach toward Thornspire.

We arrived at the next town just before noon and were able to arrange for the coach ride to the mine at Thornspire. Vagid and I rested on the ride. At dusk, the coach pulled up to an inn. Vagid and I had dinner in the common room, then went to our room.

In the morning, we had breakfast in the common room, then got back on the coach. It was another day of rest on the coach. At dusk, the coach stopped at another inn, then dinner in the common room, and off to our room.

*Coach. Inn. Common room. Bed. I'm starting to miss being chased by assassins.*

Around lunchtime the next day, Vagid was asking me whether he should buy Mara a gift for each dinner or not. I said, "Usually just the first one."

That was when Thornspire came into view out of our window.

*The last time I saw Thornspire, I was worried about bad air and a dead miner. I didn't know I'd already been inside it.*

"Evan?" said Vagid. I had stopped talking and was staring out the window.

"Sorry. Just... remembering," I said.

The coach stopped at the spot for the mine, and Vagid and I got off, picked up our packs, and started walking to the mine. It was chilly, but not cold enough for snow.

*It's really windy today.*

As we neared the mine office, we saw Johan sitting on the porch with his ledger out. *I wonder what he's drawing today.*

Orrin was leaning against the wall of the mine office, keeping an eye on things, and Nara was sitting on the porch with the group's packs as pillows to prop her up. As we got closer, it was clear that Nara was asleep or resting.

"Hi, Orrin. Johan," I said. "Is Nara okay?"

"She used up her magic. She needs to rest," said Johan.

"What he said," said Orrin.

"How has it been going?" asked Vagid. "Can you tell what kind of progress you've been able to make?"

"About a quarter," said Johan.

"Has it rained, or have you had to just use the wind?" I asked.

"There was a day that it rained, but mostly windy," said Orrin.

"How many days have you been at it?" asked Vagid.

"Just three days," said Orrin.

"So at least another nine days," said Vagid.

*Nine more days of sand. That leaves about thirty.*

"Did you talk to the foreman?" asked Vagid.

"Yep," said Orrin. "We asked about the sand, and he said take as much as you like."

"Vagid, Evan, you made it," said Nara, who was still a bit groggy.

"Yeah. Get some rest. We can talk later," I said.

"Do you have a camp?" asked Vagid.

"It's not far. While we were out working, Nara ran out of magic, and this was closer," said Orrin. "Johan can show you."

"Hey, Johan, what are you drawing now?" I asked.

Johan turned the ledger around. It showed Nara with swirling winds around her.

"We'll go get lunch ready," said Vagid. "Johan, lead the way."

Johan led Vagid and me to the campsite they were using.

"Johan, can you hunt using your magic? Like rabbits and other animals, for food?" I asked.

"Yes," said Johan. Then he turned and walked into the woods. A few minutes later, he returned with two rabbits. He handed them to me, and I took them over to Vagid. He dressed the rabbits and spitted them over the fire.

Around ten minutes later, Orrin and Nara walked into camp.

"You always show up when there's a meal, Orrin," said Vagid.

"I'm a growing boy," said Orrin.

"With as much as you eat," said Nara. "You're growing out and not up."

"Hey," said Orrin. "That's not very nice."

Vagid cut a leg off one of the rabbits and took it to Nara.

"Thank you," said Nara.

As we ate, I asked, "How have you been doing, Nara?"

"It's been difficult. At first, we tried to get the sand straight from the mine to the entrance," she said. "But the distance is too great. Johan suggested that we could carry it in buckets up the mountain so that it was closer."

"Good idea," I said, looking over at Johan.

"It was yours," he said. "I remembered the story of how Nara carved the message."

"What about the water?" asked Vagid. "Will you be able to pull it up quickly enough to be useful?"

"I can feel the groundwater below and off to the side of the chamber," said Johan. "It's a lot of water, but it will have to come through the rocks and dirt to the chamber before I can use it."

"Can't you drill it like you did at the river?" I asked. "If it's already there, just have it drill into the chamber."

Johan looked at me, surprised. "Yes, of course. And I can do that while Nara is moving the sand."

"Will it make a lot of noise?" asked Vagid.

"There are several paces of rock and dirt to get through," said Johan. "It won't get noisy until the last three or four."

"If we knew what the guard rotations were inside the chamber," I said. "We could try to time it when they were not around."

"I can feel how they move," said Johan. "Even in the chamber."

"Then drill it now, but stop before the noise starts," said Vagid. "We'll finish it when we go in."

"Once they bring in the prisoners, the chamber should be a lot noisier. Guards moving around, the prisoners, the mages making preparations," I said. "We should be able to do a lot of the final drilling as soon as they all show up."

Vagid nodded. "Good point."

"How will we know when they bring in the prisoners?" asked Orrin.

"I will know," said Johan.

I saw Nara's hand drift toward her knife. Then it stopped, and she picked up her rabbit leg instead.
