# Millbrook — Afternoon Class

The assassin arrived in Millbrook on the morning coach. He had a small bag for his belongings and a long list of names. Twelve, Thirteen, each member of Plan B, their families, and two Guild receptionists. He thought it would take most of the day to complete, possibly even tomorrow. The only names on the list that did not have an address were Twelve and Thirteen, but he had detailed descriptions of them, so it shouldn't be a problem.

The receptionists would be at work. He would start with the others. The members of Plan B. Each had their own home, except the newest member, who lived at Amaranth's chapel. He checked each address. Cold hearths, shuttered windows. At the chapel, a priest pulling weeds in the garden never looked up. The assassin decided not to go inside a god's house.

It could be that Plan B was out on a Guild job, which would delay his departure, but he would still be able to take care of the other names.

Next, the families. He started down the list of addresses, and again each was empty, and their neighbors had no idea where they had gone. Every one of them. Someone had warned them. He looked at the list again and went to the end. If anyone had done the warning, it was the two women at the Guild.

He walked to a café across the street from the Guild. He sat down at a table that gave him an excellent view of the counter where the receptionists worked. He spotted the one with braided auburn hair, and the strawberry blonde. While he watched, he ordered tea. For several hours they dealt with adventurers who would queue up in their lines. The blonde-haired one seemed very perky, while the auburn-haired one seemed bored.

Lunchtime came and went, and the assassin ordered another cup of tea. Still the receptionists worked, handling whatever the people in their lines brought them. Neither of them stopped to eat. *Disciplined,* he thought. *For receptionists.*

At midafternoon, they both took a break and exited the Guild. They walked down the street beside the Guild.

The assassin paid for the cups of tea and followed at a discreet distance, making sure to only use his peripheral vision to track them. The women went to the training grounds behind the Guild and joined some other people. The assassin continued to walk past the training grounds until he reached an alleyway, then turned down it. He walked to the midpoint of the alleyway, checked for anyone watching, then deftly climbed to the roof of the building. He didn't make any sound as he walked across the roof to the parapet. He peeked over the edge, looking at the people in the training ground. They all seemed to be taking direction from a stooped old woman with a cane. As he watched, she poked a large man in the chest with one finger, and he went down like a sack of grain. The assassin almost smiled. The students were humoring her.

He watched the receptionists train. He noticed that the blonde-haired one was quick, but fought like she was playing. The auburn-haired one was not as quick as the blonde, but was still a very strong fighter.

The assassin decided to wait until the class ended. He could follow the women to their homes and kill each one there.

He looked around the training area. Something was missing, but what?

The old woman. Where had she gone? She had been there just a minute ago. He thought of the finger, and the large man going down, and suddenly he understood.

"I'm not overly fond of people spying on my classes," he heard a voice say from behind him.

He turned around, pulling a knife to throw. But his hand dropped the knife for some reason. His whole arm was hanging limply. He reached for a knife with his other hand, but never made it. The old woman seemed to shift her hand holding her cane—no, she had hit him with her cane. It was so fast he wasn't able to follow the movement. He couldn't breathe or speak. He toppled over like a marionette with the strings cut.

He heard his heartbeat in his ears. BaDum, BaDum, BaDum. The old woman had disappeared too. BaDum, BaDum. He could hear her farther away, talking to the class. "Class is over. Same time tomorrow. Mara, Prue, I have something for you to deal with." BaDum.

Then silence.
