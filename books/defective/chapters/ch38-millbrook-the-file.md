# Millbrook — The File

General Garrick Ashford sat at a café table across the street from the Millbrook Guild, sipping tea. He watched two soldiers he had sent walk into the Guild. The soldiers joined the line of a young, perky-looking woman. When they reached the front of the line, Garrick could see them ask the woman something, then she turned, picked up a stack of papers that was already waiting, and handed it over. The smile never left her face. The woman waved to the soldiers as they left.

*Nobody that cheerful is** that slow.*

The soldiers left the Guild and walked past the café where Garrick sat. One of them passed close enough to brush his chair and didn't look twice. Garrick had commanded the man for three years.

Garrick quietly finished his tea. He went into the Guild, joined the line of a woman who looked bored, and waited. The woman's auburn hair was braided and ran to her waist. She was very pretty and only a bit older than the perky woman.

Once he reached the front of the line, the woman was writing something down. "Yeah?" she asked, without looking up.

"I'd like to see Plan B's file," said Garrick.

The woman's expression never changed, but her pen stopped. She looked up at Garrick, then said, "Let me get it."

The woman walked up the stairs. A few minutes later, she returned with a folder stuffed with papers. "Per Guild policy, you are not permitted to remove it from the Guild, but you can review it in one of our meeting rooms."

The woman led Garrick to a small empty room with a table and four chairs. She put the folder down on the table in front of one of the chairs, then left.

Garrick sat at the table with the door facing him and opened the file.

On top were several pages of the Otherborn writing. He couldn't read any of it, but he didn't like seeing it here. *It doesn't point to anyone. But it means an Otherborn is working with Plan B.*

Under them were copies of documents Garrick knew well. Treasury writs. Requisition orders. A page from the summoning ledger. Each one had been stamped by the Guild and filed, as neatly as a tax receipt.

*These came from my archive.*

Behind the ledger page were reports Garrick had never seen. Core trials, each with a date and a single word. *Expired. Escaped. Ruptured.*

*I signed for the summonings. Nobody told me about this.*

The last page was a single sheet in the same Otherborn hand. The words were strange, but the numbers were in Hallish. *Thirteen. Four hundred.* He didn't need to read the rest. He had filed the original himself.

Someone had copied it out by hand. Out of everything in the archive, they had copied this one.

*Thirteen walked out of that river.*

Garrick closed the folder and sat with his hand flat on top of it for a long moment.

Then he left it on the table and walked out. The bored woman didn't look up as he passed, but her pen had stopped again.

As Garrick walked away from the Guild, a man who had sat at the café for three hours paid for one cup of tea and caught the evening coach to Lumenhold.

Silas didn't look up from his desk. "How long was he inside?"

"Half an hour," said the man. "His soldiers got their list from a cheerful one. He asked a different one for something. She went upstairs for it like she'd been waiting for someone to ask."

Silas set down his pen.

*Half an hour is a long time to read a file you plan to report.*

"Has the general sent word?"

"No, sir."

"No," Silas said. "I didn't think he would. Go down to the cells. Tell them to release Twelve."

The man hesitated. "Sir, the general had him—"

"The general is busy." Silas picked his pen back up. "Tell Twelve the Guild in Millbrook has two clerks who keep very good files. I'd like him to see to them."

The man left. Silas went back to his writing. The only sound in the room was the scratch of his pen.
