# Chapter 29: It's About Time

I ran from the Guild to the cafe where Plan B meets for breakfast. Honestly, it was more a stumbling trot with my leg still giving me pain.

I was late getting there for our breakfast meeting, but luckily they were still there.

"Evan, where have you been?" asked Nara. "We've been waiting on you."

"I started training at the Guild in the mornings, at dawn," I said. "And I told Prue about my origins and stuff."

"You told Prue?" asked Vagid. "Why would you do that?"

"It's typical for boyfriends to share things like that with their girlfriends," I said.

Nara smiled. "I'm happy for you," she said, and gave me a hug.

"Way to go," said Orrin. "It was the sword poses I taught you, right?"

"Girlfriend?" asked Vagid.

"But Vagid's question is valid," said Nara. "Even though you are growing close, doesn't that put her in danger?"

"That is another part of what you need to know. Soldiers came to the Guild. They were asking about people who recently registered with the Guild," I said. "Mara and Prue stalled them, but only for a week."

Nara put down her mug and looked straight at me. "I asked about Prue."

Orrin stopped chewing.

"I was going to run away and not even ask," I said. "But in the end, this relationship is important to me, so I asked what Prue wanted."

Orrin swallowed his food and said, "That's actually really mature."

"Girlfriend?" said Vagid. *I think my confession broke him.*

Nara was still staring at me. Then she gave a little nod and said, "It's about time."

Suddenly, Vagid came back to his senses and said, "Who are you and what have you done with Evan?"

*Well, at least he wasn't broken anymore.*

"Where did we look in that room?" I asked.

"Nara took the wall we came through, I took the one opposite the door, and you took the one to the right of the door," said Vagid.

"I listened at the door for anyone coming in," said Orrin.

"So what was left?" asked Nara.

Vagid suddenly hit his forehead with the heel of his palm. "The wall where the door is. There were shelves there too."

I filled Plan B in on what Prue had told me and how it made me realize that we had missed the same thing.

Orrin said, "I was only supposed to listen for people coming in." He seemed to have missed the point, but that was normal.

"The one place we didn't even look," said Nara. "Of course."

"What do we expect to find?" asked Vagid. "Do we know?"

"I don't know, but it's the only place left and we're out of clues," I said.

"I don't think we should rely on Tobin this time," said Vagid. "The kobold job is done, and we told Tobin there were no more kobolds."

"I don't want to put Tobin and his family at risk," said Nara.

"Is there a town near Lumenhold that the coach stops at?" I asked. "Wherever we go we'll need some reason to be there."

"The Guild," said Vagid. "We should check to see if there are jobs in the area of Lower Lumenhold."

"You'll have to ask Mara," said Orrin, grinning. "And you should ask her to dinner while you're there."

Vagid's ears and face turned red at that. "I'll ask about the job. The other will have to wait."

We walked over to the Guild. Nara, Orrin and I sat at a table near the Guild and ordered something to drink. Orrin made sure we had a good view of Mara's line. Vagid went inside to ask about jobs, and we watched him join the line.

"Hi, Vagid! It's so good to see you again!" said Orrin in his best imitation of Mara.

Then Orrin switched to a deeper voice and said, "Hi, Mara. I was wondering if there were any jobs near Lower Lumenhold for a strapping young man such as myself?"

I coughed up my tea, and Nara softly chuckled.

"Oh, Vagid, you're so manly. I have just the job for you," said Orrin in his Mara voice. "It's only at night and it's at my place."

This time, Nara coughed up her tea, and I was laughing so hard I almost fell out of my chair.

"Sorry, Mara, I need to stay focused on my friends. But I can always stop by if you need help with anything... anything," said Orrin as Vagid.

"I'll cry myself to sleep until then. But here is a job in some small village," said Orrin as Mara. We saw Mara hand Vagid a job slip. "I can't wait until our night tryst."

*Does Orrin even know what a tryst is? And where did he hear about it?*

A moment later, Vagid came out of the Guild and joined us at our table.

"Sedgewick," said Vagid. "The hamlet of Sedgewick has a goblin problem. Less than ten goblins. The request is less than a week old."

"And dinner?" asked Orrin, grinning.

"I said it would have to wait," said Vagid. "Don't you have something better to do?"

"Sedgewick is close enough to walk to the hill with the caves," said Nara. "We can reach the tunnel without anyone in Lower Lumenhold seeing us. Tobin never has to know."

"What about the goblins?" asked Orrin.

"If they get in our way, we deal with them," said Vagid.

Orrin looked a little too happy about that.

"Nara, Evan, and Orrin get supplies. I'll arrange the coach rides," said Vagid. "I assume you can afford the ride and a cushion, Evan."

"Yeah, I still have money left over from the last few jobs," I said. "No need for a loan."

We split up to cover our tasks. We picked up jerky, hard bread and, after some internal debate, cheese. I tried not to look guilty buying it. Then more candles for the lanterns. We met back up with Vagid near the Guild. He had made the coach arrangements.

"The coach will leave at noon today," said Vagid. "If you have anything else you need to do, you have about an hour to get it done."

I went into the Guild and joined Prue's line. "Are you normally this clingy?" asked Prue when I got to the front.

"How would I know? I just wanted to say I'll be out of town for a few days. Also, can you let Stella know that I'm not wimping out on her training?" I asked.

"I can tell her," said Prue. "Have fun."

I joined Plan B at the coach stop in Millbrook. We loaded our gear and took our seats, with cushions. As the coach departed, I leaned over to Vagid and asked, "There's less than a week before they return. If we can't find something helpful, then I'll probably need to stay out of town for a while."

"Is that a decision for you, or someone else?" asked Vagid.

*Oh. Right.* "...Someone else," I said. "Everyone else, probably."

I leaned back in the coach and closed my eyes.

*Tired Guy, we're heading back to the archive,* I said. *I just wanted you to know.*

*Thank you, Evan. Be careful.*
