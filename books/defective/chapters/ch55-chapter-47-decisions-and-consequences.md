# Chapter 47: Decisions and Consequences

"There are many more prisoners than before," said Johan. "They are being put into the holding cells in the cave."

"Orrin, Evan, we need to get a look at this," said Vagid.

We slipped through the forest to where we could see the entrance. There were more tents being erected. They were being put in neat rows, so they were probably guards. There was also a coach, with a coat of arms painted on the door.

We returned to the camp.

"How close are we with the amount of sand we need?" asked Vagid.

"Halfway. Maybe," I said.

"We go in with what we have," said Nara.

"There's no choice," said Vagid.

Orrin nodded.

"Johan, keep drilling, but not all the way through yet," said Vagid. "Nara, rest up. We need you once we're in the cave."

Vagid looked at me. "Evan, you and I will keep moving the sand."

Then at Orrin. "Orrin, you need to keep an eye on the guards and the entrance. If anything changes, let us know. How many arrows do you have?"

"Half a quiver, so about fifteen," said Orrin.

"Make them count," said Vagid.

"We'll meet back here when it's closer to dinnertime."

We split up for our tasks.

By dinnertime, Vagid and I were steaming shirtless again. Orrin and Johan returned, and we had a cold dinner of jerky and cheese.

"We'll go in at the dawn guard change. Johan, you will need to guide us past the guards," said Vagid.

"I will be ready," said Johan.

"Once we're in the cave, we will go straight to the chamber, dealing with any guards we run into," said Vagid.

"What about the prisoners?" said Orrin.

"As long as they have value as sacrifices, they're not going to kill them," said Vagid. "They're safest in the holding cells."

Nara didn't look happy about that, but it was hard to argue with Vagid's logic.

"When we've killed the guards in the chamber, Orrin and I will go back and hold the entrance," said Vagid. "As long as we can."

"What about me?" I asked.

"You'll stay in the chamber with Nara and Johan," said Vagid. "You still haven't gotten good enough with your sword to be in a fight like this."

*Fair point.*

"Get what sleep you can," said Vagid. Orrin, Johan, and Nara all lay down. Nara had made sure her bedroll was on the opposite side of the fire from Johan. No one said anything about it.

I started stretching when Vagid said, "I miss Mara. We've only been to dinner a few times, but I keep thinking about her."

"Yeah. I miss Prue, too," I said. "When you think of your life before you went out with Mara, what word comes to mind?"

"Desolate," said Vagid.

"And now that you're going out with her?"

"Wonderful," he said.

"Good," I said. "You should tell her that."

Vagid looked at me for a minute, then went to bed to try and sleep.

Orrin was tossing and turning. *Guess we're all nervous about tomorrow.*

When my watch was up, I woke Johan.

I got to sleep and dreamed we won, and I chose to go back. Johan was forced to stay behind and eventually died alone, Prue eventually found someone else, everyone in Plan B settled down and had their own families. I went back, but I regretted it every day for the rest of my life. I woke groggy with sleep in my eyes. I looked over to see Vagid sitting by the fire. *It's close to time to get up. Vagid's watch is the last one.* I got up and joined Vagid by the fire.

"Trouble sleeping?" asked Vagid.

"Bad dream," I said.

"Any progress on your decision?" asked Vagid.

"Not really... maybe," I said. *I'm still not sure.*

"Well, it's time to get everyone up," said Vagid.

"As if we could actually sleep," said Orrin from his bedroll.

"It's difficult," said Nara.

"Yes," said Johan.

We broke camp and had jerky for breakfast.

"Johan, you'll guide us. Nara, you'll make some noise with the wind to cover us," said Vagid. "Let's go save the world."

We all nodded and headed for the entrance.

We were behind the guard tents when Johan held up his hand and stopped. The night watch walked out toward the camp for breakfast.

After they passed our location, Johan pointed to Nara. She nodded and sent a gust across the camp: tent flaps snapped, someone swore and chased a hat. Johan motioned, and we set off for the entrance. Orrin ducked past a tent pole and came back with a second quiver. *Was that... a quiver?* Orrin grinned.

We made it into the entrance.

*Tired Guy?* I asked.

*I am here, Evan. I will be wait*—

Then nothing. *For the first time since the river, I was alone in my head.*

In the cave, we walked down the path, passing rows and rows of holding cells. In one, a small girl in a torn dress slept against the bars. We were past the holding cells when Johan's hand went up. We all froze. He quickly pointed back to a side passage. *More like a crack in the cave wall.*

We all squeezed into the crack. It was a very tight fit. We heard heavy footsteps coming back up the path.

"Damn boots," a guard said, then stopped right beside the crack to tie his boot. Just as the guard knelt, my nose started to itch. I pinched my nose closed and hoped I didn't actually sneeze. Orrin saw me holding my nose with my eyes watering. He looked like he was staring at a bomb about to go off.

As he stood up, he scratched himself and spit off to the side. Then he continued up the path to the entrance.

We clawed our way out of the crack, then continued down the path to the chamber.

At the door to the chamber, Johan held up his hand and we all stopped. He held up two fingers and pointed to the right side. Then held up one finger and pointed to the left side.

Vagid tapped Orrin on the shoulder and they disappeared into the chamber. We heard a scuffle, then silence. Orrin came back out and motioned us in.

When I entered the chamber, the first thing I saw was the table. The one I had been tortured on. I froze, staring at it.

"Nara, begin gathering the sand. Johan, finish drilling. Evan, keep them safe. Orrin, let's go," said Vagid.

When I heard my name, I nodded and snapped out of my paralysis. *This has to go too.*

Nara raised her hands and a wind sprang up in the chamber. *This is like when she cleaned the barracks we stayed in, just in reverse.* The sand began to pile up beside Nara. After all the work we did, the pile of sand looked too small. *This won't be enough.*

The drilling noise was getting louder, until a tendril of water poked through the floor. Then more tendrils poked through. It looked like an octopus made of water was under the floor.

Johan turned to Nara and asked, "May I?"

Nara waited a second before saying, "Yes."

All of the tendrils scooped up the sand and started to work. The drilling noise grew louder.

Nara leaned against the table to catch her breath. She looked at it, then at me, and as if realizing what the table was used for, took her hand off it and wiped it on her cloak.

I took her arm to make sure she was okay.

"Nara, catch your breath," I said. "Johan, stick to the pattern. Some of the tendrils are going outside of it."

Johan nodded. I could see sweat beading on his forehead and a vein standing out. *Not tired. Concentrating.*

Above the drilling noise, I heard a bell chiming. "That's the alarm," I said. *Good luck, Vagid and Orrin.*

"Nara, will you be okay? I'm going over to the doorway. If anyone gets past Vagid and Orrin, I'll do my best to slow them down," I said.

"Be careful, Evan," said Nara.

I nodded and ran over to the doorway. *I really wish I had learned more from Stella.*

I heard Orrin's bow, *Thrum*, then some swords clanging. *Thrum*. Pause. *Thrum*. More swords. *Thrum*. Sword clanging, cries of pain. *Thrum*, *Thrum*. Cries of pain. In the distance, I could hear voices giving orders, but they were too far away to understand what they said.

This bizarre symphony continued for several minutes.

Then I heard another *Thrum*.

And Vagid yelled, "I thought you said fifteen?"

"That other quiver was just sitting there. Who would miss it?" yelled back Orrin.

The symphony continued for longer this time.

*Thrum.* "I'm out," yelled Orrin.

The sound of running feet. Vagid and Orrin appeared out of the darkness.

"Status!" said Vagid.

I ran over to Johan. He was visibly straining now. Sweat pouring down his face, his arms outstretched in front of him, guiding the tendrils. I looked out at the pattern. There in the middle was a different-colored stone that all the silver lines ran toward.

"Johan, take out that stone in the middle," I said. "Maybe combine some of the tendrils so there are less to control."

Nara started to use her winds to pull in all the stray sand in the chamber. She was leaning on the table again. She didn't seem to notice.

I went back to Vagid and Orrin. "They're doing it, but it's taking longer than we thought."

"Damn. Okay, Evan, come with us. We'll hold the door as long as we can," said Vagid.

*So much for staying out of the fight.*

I heard boots. A large number all stepping in concert. Then the sound of metal bumping on metal.

There were torches coming down the corridor. Then I could see what was making that noise. A classic shield wall. The corridor wasn't wide enough for more than three guards across, but there were lots and lots of rows of guards.

Then, in the back, I saw Fancy Guard, Bored Dude, and Ball Guy.

"Dorran?" I heard Vagid whisper. Then I saw Vagid's brother Dorran standing behind Fancy Guard.

There was no way we could stop a shield wall, so we backed up. I glanced back. Nara was still pulling sand. She hadn't even looked up. The wall split, and the three people I least wanted to see again walked forward.

Bored Dude looked past us at Johan. "Twelve? What are you doing?"

Then he looked down at the floor. The pattern was half sand. For the first time since the table, Bored Dude wasn't bored at all. "Stop him!"

Two guards grabbed Johan. The tendrils went still.

Ball Guy raised his hand. He still had the ball in it. *Does he sleep with it?*

"Appraise!" he said. "No abilities. None." He went pale. "Thirteen?"

"Ah, Thirteen. The gods must favor me, to have brought you back. Before, you were useless, but now you have found a use. As a sacrifice. For Fourteen." He turned to the guards. "Put them in with the other units. Not Twelve. Twelve comes with me."

I looked at Johan and Nara. Johan's face, which had been expressionless for so long, was now showing hatred and fury.

"No," said Johan. "Never again!"

"What?" said Bored Dude. "You dare?"

The guards who were holding Johan's arms were suddenly missing their heads. *The tendrils?* Two of the tendrils had slashed their heads while they were still spinning. The bodies crumpled.

"Grand cyclone," I heard Nara say from behind me. And the same type of cyclone that had killed the goblins at the farm appeared around the first few rows of guards. The goblins were much lighter than the guards, but the wind in the chamber was filled with sand. The result was the guards being slowly flayed.

"Keep... destroying... the pattern," gasped Nara to Johan.

He turned back to the tendrils and combined several until only four remained. They were much thicker. The drilling resumed on the center stone.

Vagid, Orrin, and I moved to take any guards that escaped the cyclone and killed them as quickly as we could. *Don't think. I can't afford to.*

"Stop them!" screamed Bored Dude. "Push through!"

Nara's cyclone was getting smaller and smaller. I looked back at her and saw that she had fallen to the floor but was still feeding her magic into the cyclone.

Suddenly the cyclone fell apart. I looked back and saw Nara lying there unmoving.

Then there was a sound like hitting two rocks together, but a hundred times louder. We all fell to our knees, holding our ears.

*Evan!* I heard his voice.

*Tired Guy? How?* I asked.

*You did it, Evan. The chamber is destroyed.*

*But... Nara...* I said.

I stumbled back to where Nara was lying. She was barely breathing.

*No! Why? Not Nara.* I said. *She used all of her magic. What can I do?*

"Kill them! Kill them!" screamed Bored Dude. "Kill—"

I looked over to Bored Dude. Fancy Guard was standing slightly behind him. There was a sword point sticking out of Bored Dude's chest. *Fancy Guard? Did you?*

Fancy Guard turned to Dorran and said clearly, "The mages."

Dorran hesitated, then understood. He turned to the guards near him. "Not one mage leaves here alive."

*Evan. It is time. You must decide,* said Tired Guy.

"But Nara," I said.

*Evan.*

*I can be such an idiot at times. I've been agonizing over a decision when I had already decided.*

"I'm staying," I said.

*Evan. Nara still has a chance.*

"What? How? What can I do?" I said.

"Evan, who are you talking to?" asked Vagid.

"Maybe his god?" said Orrin.

*My name. A god without a name does not have power to give to his acolytes.*

I was stumped. What name could I give a god? Then I remembered Walt. *Grab an eddy. They're the rest stops of the river.*

"Eddy," I said.

There was a sound like an enormous bell ringing. The entire chamber shook with the sound.

*Now, Evan. You are my acolyte, and I can lend you my power. Use High Heal.*

"High Heal!" I said. A glowing circle spread out from where I was sitting with Nara. It kept going and going until it reached the wounded guards. Then each of the wounded began to glow. Their flayed skin regrew. I looked down. Nara was awake and staring at me.

"Evan, what did you do?" she asked.

"I guess I'm a cleric now. Of a new god named Eddy," I said.

"But, Evan, there isn't a High Heal. No cleric can cast it, because it doesn't exist," said Nara.

"Huh. It does now," I said.

"Eddy, send Johan back," I said.

"But Evan, I cannot go back! I am an old man!" said Johan.

"Then live a new life," I said. "A better life."

Johan came over to me and hugged me. "Thank you, Evan. In the long time that I have been here, you are my first friend."

*The others are here. They have been waiting.*

A circle of light appeared on the floor near us.

*Johan must step into the circle to go back.*

"There's your chance. Live a good life," I said.

Johan looked at each of us. Nara met his eyes and nodded once.

Johan stepped into the circle, tears freely flowing down his face.

There was a bright flash, then he was gone.

*It is done. The wall is sealed. No one will ever cross again.*

I helped Nara stand, then walked toward the guards. They parted like the Red Sea when I got close. I faced Fancy Guard and said, "Hi. I'm Evan McIntyre. You used to know me as Thirteen."

"Garrick Ashford," said Fancy Guard.

"I would like to heal any of your men that need it. May I?" I asked.

Garrick turned to Dorran. "Take him to the wounded immediately."

I ran over to Dorran, who led me back up the corridor.

I thought I heard Garrick say, "We can talk later."

Dorran and I left the entrance and went to the medical tent. There were more than fifty wounded, some in very dire circumstances. I went to the center of the tent, took a deep breath, and said, "High Heal."

The circle snapped out to cover the entire tent and everyone inside began to glow. Missing limbs regrew, open cuts closed, missing eyes grew back. *Truly miraculous.*

Then I felt dizzy and nauseous. Everything suddenly tilted to the side, then blackness.
