# Chapter 48: Not Just Tonight

"He'll wake up. He just used all his energy." I heard Nara's voice, but it was muffled.

"Post guards around him. I don't want anyone bothering him while he recovers." *Was that Garrick?*

*Evan,* said a voice. It was familiar, but normally it sounded tired.

*Tired Guy? No. Not anymore. Eddy?*

*Yes, Evan. I know you are tired, but you must wake up. There is something that we need to do,* said Eddy.

"Uuuhhh." I got one eye open. I could see Nara sitting beside the cot I was in.

"Evan, you need to rest," said Nara.

"Nope." I rested a second. "No rest for... the weary."

Then a longer pause. "Eddy needs me... to do... something," I said.

Nara looked worried, but she turned to the guards. "You and you. Help him stand up," she said.

They reached out and helped me stand up. Really, they were just holding me up. I was wiped out.

"What does the god Eddy need?" asked Nara.

*Eddy, where am I going?* I asked.

*Go back to the chamber,* said Eddy.

"Chamber," I said.

"Quickly, take him to the chamber," said Nara.

The guards carried me to the chamber.

*Eddy, we're there. Now what?* I asked.

*The center stone was hiding something. The other gods and I want to know what it says.*

I looked around for Nara. She was there, and apparently we had collected Vagid, Orrin, Dorran, and Garrick too.

"There was... a center stone... clear the pieces..." I said.

"Do it," said Garrick to Dorran. Dorran pointed to several guards and directed them as they cleared the pieces of the center stone.

"Sir! It's done, sir!"

"Evan, you aren't in any shape for this," said Orrin.

"All of the gods... want to know... what is under that," I said.

*Evan. This is a message for all. People and gods. We will help you to read the message, but it may be painful. I am sorry for that,* said Eddy.

"Yeah. Okay. Take me over to it."

The guards carried me over to the odd design that was under the center stone.

*Are you ready, Evan?* asked Eddy.

"Yeah," I said. Then the worst migraine I had ever had hit me, and I threw up. Once I got my stomach under control, I looked at the design again. I could read it now. I read the message out loud.

> To the ones who break this stone:
>
> I made this place in a dark year, to bring one hero across the wall and save my people. It worked. So they used it again. And again. I told myself each time was the last.
>
> I never asked the ones I brought. I took them as one takes water from a well.
>
> The wall wore thin. To save both worlds, we gods wiped this one clean, and we called it mercy. It was not mercy. It was the only thing left that we could do.
>
> I hid these words where no one who used this place could ever read them. If you are reading them, you have done what I could not.
>
> Thank you. Forgive me, if you can.
>
> —Ysmeric

*No one has spoken that name since before I existed,* said Eddy.

*They will remember him now. The chamber is gone and the wall is sealed. There will be no reset.*

Nobody spoke. Then Nara said quietly, "He said thank you."

"Please take me back to the medical tent," I said.

I spent the next two days sleeping. I would hear voices but was just too tired to wake up.

When I did finally wake up, I was starving. I asked for two steaks. They brought me stew.

*Eddy?* I asked.

*Yes, Evan,* he said.

*You don't sound tired anymore,* I said.

*I am not. You did that.*

*Me? What did I do?* I asked.

*You gave me a name, and believed in me.*

*Thank you, Eddy,* I said.

I was able to move around some, but still got tired easily. I went looking for Plan B and found them sitting around a fire.

"Hey," I said.

"Evan, should you be up?" asked Nara.

"At least he's not throwing up," said Orrin.

"Hey, Evan," said Vagid.

"I was thinking about something," I said.

"What?" asked Vagid.

"We wasted our time moving the sand," I said. "When Johan started drilling toward the chamber, it created more sand than we were able to."

"So the hole made its own sand?" asked Orrin.

"Yes," I said.

"Huh. I know stuff and I'm right," said Orrin.

"One out of two isn't too bad," said Vagid.

Nara sighed. "Johan told us. We'd be making our own."

"Excuse me," said a voice from behind me. I turned around and there stood Garrick.

"Hi, Garrick. Please join us," I said.

Garrick took a seat beside Orrin. "I would ask a favor of you, Evan."

"Sure, what can I do?" I asked.

"The prisoners," said Garrick. "Many of them have medical issues. Some are beyond anything our healers can handle."

"Of course," I said. "I may need you to help me get there. I haven't gotten my strength back yet."

"Yes," said Garrick. He got up, then helped me stand and held onto my arm as we walked to the extended area where they had set up tents for the prisoners.

"What will be done with these people?" I asked.

"I do not know yet," said Garrick. "I have sent to the capital to ask the King to send help, but I have not heard a response yet."

Then he paused for a moment.

"I should warn you. There are five hundred prisoners," said Garrick.

"Whoa, that's a lot. Let me check with Eddy," I said.

*Eddy?* I asked.

*Yes, Evan?* asked Eddy.

*I've been asked to heal a large group of people. The prisoners. Can I do that without passing out?*

*Yes. Your capacity for magic has been growing. You should be able to cast High Heal several times now.*

"Wow. That's good," I said.

"Was Eddy able to help?" asked Garrick.

"Yeah. He said that I should be able to cast High Heal several times now," I said. "Are they still prisoners?"

"No. I issued the order to free them while you were recovering," said Garrick.

"That's good," I said. "You're a good man under that gruff exterior."

We reached the first set of tents where the prisoners were staying.

"This is going to sound like an odd question, but do you know how far my High Heal extends?" I asked.

"The guards outside of the medical tent said the circle extended another twenty paces beyond the tent wall," said Garrick. "That would put it at around thirty-five paces." *Over eighty feet in every direction? That's insane!*

"Okay. Can I leave it to you to figure out where I need to be to heal all of them?" I asked.

"Of course," said Garrick.

He took me to a location, then said, "This is the first spot."

I took a deep breath. "High Heal."

Again, the circle spread out with me at its center. Everyone who was injured inside the circle began to glow. When the glow faded, they were whole again.

Garrick held up his hand and a group of guards appeared around me. "The high cleric still needs to heal others today. Please wait to thank him until after he has finished."

They led me to the next area and I repeated the process. We did this three more times. On the last one, I was starting to feel lightheaded. Garrick must have noticed when I put more of my weight on his arm. He turned to two of the guards and said, "That is enough for today. Please help Evan back to the medical tent and arrange for him to have dinner."

The guards helped me back to the tent. More stew, then sleep.

The next day, I was feeling better and stronger.

*Eddy?* I asked.

*Yes, Evan,* he said.

*I don't know how to be a cleric or priest. Is there something special I need to do?*

*You are doing fine. Just be yourself.*

*Thank you,* I said.

I got up from the cot and wasn't too unsteady. So I decided to go find Plan B before going back to heal the prisoners.

I found them in a tent near the medical tent.

"Good morning," I said.

"You look like you're doing better," said Vagid.

"Yep." I sat in a chair. "I wanted to say thank you to all of you."

"For what?" asked Nara.

"For finding me. Being my friends. Helping me," I said.

"Sure," said Orrin.

"I think I have another round of healing with the not-prisoners today, but after that, would it be okay if we headed back to Millbrook?" I asked.

"We can catch the coach at the mine. We should ask Garrick to loan us a guard or two to go back with us," said Vagid.

"Why would he do that?" I asked.

They all looked at me shocked.

"What? I don't get it," I said.

"Evan, word of what you can do has already spread to the capital. The Guild has been swamped with people asking how they can get you to heal them," said Nara.

"Really? I didn't think it was that big a deal," I said.

"I told you that no one can do High Heal," said Nara. "And it grows back limbs! That's absurd! And you're the only one who can do it."

"Wait, how do you know about the Guild in the capital?" I asked.

"Garrick told me when he came by to check on you," said Nara.

"Oh..." I said.

"Sir! General Ashford would like to see you. Sir!" said Dorran, who was standing just outside the tent.

"Hi, Dorran. Okay, lead the way," I said.

Dorran walked toward a larger tent, and I followed.

"Dorran, have you and Vagid had a chance to talk?" I asked.

"Sir! Yes, sir!" said Dorran.

I sighed and stopped walking. Dorran noticed that I wasn't following and came back.

"Dorran, this is going to be a lot easier if you would just treat me like you do Orrin or Nara. I'm just a friend of your family," I said.

He stepped closer and whispered, "Evan, General Ashford has told all the guards to treat you as if you were a general too. So, sorry, but get over it."

"Yeah. Okay," I said.

Inside, Garrick sat at a camp table covered in papers. A little girl sat on a cot beside him, swinging her feet, eating bread. Her dress was clean now, but I knew her. The girl asleep against the bars.

"I didn't know you had a daughter," I said.

"I do," said Garrick. "She is at home. She makes horses out of clay." He almost smiled. "Badly."

He nodded at the girl. "This is Mica. She was the youngest in the cells. There is a girl in Lumenhold who has been asking for her."

"Hello, Mica," I said.

Mica stared at me, still eating the bread. *Young kids are often shy.*

"What can I do for you, General?" I asked.

"Evan, please call me Garrick," he said. "And I would like to assign bodyguards to you while you are here."

"Who would want to hurt me here?" I asked.

"I do not believe anyone in this camp would want to hurt you," said Garrick. "But you are the only person able to cast High Heal. There might be people who would want to kidnap you."

"Really?" I asked.

"Yes," said Garrick.

"Okay. Can we take them with us to Millbrook? I want to get back there soon," I said.

"Of course," said Garrick. "Dorran, can you make the arrangements?"

"Sir! Yes, sir!"

"I still need to heal the rest of the people from yesterday that I couldn't get to," I said.

"Yes, there are matters that I must attend to today. Dorran, can you escort Evan on his rounds?"

"Sir! Yes, sir!"

"Thank you, Garrick," I said.

"Goodbye, Mica," I said and waved to her.

"You're the glowy man," said Mica. I stopped and turned around.

"Yep," I said. "If you ever get hurt again, come find me. I'll help you."

She nodded. "Bye."

Dorran repurposed two guards and led the way to the last spot that I healed yesterday, and then past it by close to one hundred and sixty feet.

"Sir! Here. Sir!" said Dorran.

I cast High Heal and watched it work. We repeated this process four more times before we were done.

Dorran led me back, but we walked right past the medical tent.

"Uh, Dorran, we just passed the medical tent," I said.

"Sir! General Ashford has prepared a more suitable tent for you. Sir!" said Dorran.

*This ought to be good.*

We arrived at a tent that was roughly the same size as Garrick's. *Good grief.* "Isn't this a bit much?"

"Sir! No, sir!" said Dorran.

"Dorran, can you check with Vagid to see when they want to head back to Millbrook?" I said. "I know he wants to see Mara as much as I want to see Prue."

"Sir! Mara? Sir!" said Dorran.

"Yeah, he is going out with a beautiful, perky strawberry-blonde lady named Mara," I said.

"Sir! Please, excuse me, sir!" said Dorran.

He left. *I wish I could be there for that fun talk.*

I found my cot and lay down. After five High Heals, I was tired.

*Eddy, thank you for being my friend,* I said.

*You are welcome, Evan,* said Eddy.

I fell asleep.

"Hey, Evan, time to get up," said Orrin.

*What the?!*

I jerked up and fell on the floor. Then I heard Orrin laughing.

"Orrin, that wasn't nice," said Nara.

"But normal for Orrin," said Vagid.

I stood up. "I'm up," I said.

"We're all packed and ready to go," said Vagid.

"Did Dorran send the guards?" I asked.

"They are waiting outside the tent," said Orrin.

I looked around, but I didn't see any of my normal things. No bedroll. No armor. No sword.

"Where is all of my stuff?" I asked.

"Orrin, can you ask the guards where Evan's old things are?" asked Vagid.

"Sure," said Orrin, and he ducked out of the tent. I could hear him talking, then he came back in with one of the guards.

"Evan, this is Starn," said Orrin. "Go ahead and tell him."

"Sir! General Ashford has replaced the equipment you originally had. Sir!" said Starn.

"Um... why?" I asked.

"Sir! The general felt that it did not provide enough protection. Sir!" said Starn.

"So where is the stuff he replaced it with?" I asked.

Starn walked across the room and stood next to some robes that had what looked like chain mail woven into key areas.

I sighed. "Is there a changing room in this huge tent?" I asked.

"Oh, don't worry, Evan. We carried you from the river to Millbrook when you were naked. We've seen it all by now," said Nara, smirking.

Orrin, who was sitting on a stool drinking tea, spewed the tea out and laughed.

Vagid just shook his head.

"It's not like I like walking around naked, so let me get changed, then we can go. Though I'm not sure how to get into that thing."

Starn helped me get into the warrior robes. I felt ridiculous.

"Let's get going," I said. I learned that the other guard was a woman named Glarna.

We got to the mine coach drop-off spot by midmorning. The coach arrived around noon. We loaded our gear and climbed in.

I tried to sleep, but some of the other passengers kept talking about a new cleric who could heal lost limbs. Orrin would snicker, then say, "Have you heard about that, Evan?"

After a few days of this, we finally arrived back in Millbrook. It was noon, and I really wanted to see Prue, so I walked into the Guild, followed closely by Vagid, Orrin, Nara, Starn, and then Glarna.

I stopped and looked at Orrin and Nara. "Here for the show?"

"We don't want to miss the fun," said Nara.

I sighed again, then joined Prue's line. When I got to the front, I heard, "I didn't know you liked to wear dresses."

"They're surprisingly liberating," I said.

"Mara, I'm going to lunch," said Prue.

Mara looked over to me and said, "Hi, Evan! It's so good to see you again!"

Prue took me by the hand and led me to the small conference room. As Starn and Glarna were about to enter, she slammed the door.

"What did you do?" asked Prue.

"You're going to need to be more specific," I said.

"We got a notice yesterday," she said and handed me a paper.

> **BY ORDER OF HIS MAJESTY BERTRAM, KING OF SUNHALLOW**
>
> **Be it known to all subjects of the realm:**
>
> That the god known as **Eddy**, lately revealed, is hereby recognized by the Crown, and that His worship shall be permitted freely throughout Sunhallow;
>
> That **Evan McIntyre**, formerly called Thirteen, is the first and only cleric of the said god, and is hereby named **High Cleric of Eddy**, with all the honor and standing due a lord of the realm;
>
> That the High Cleric did, at Thornspire, heal more than five hundred souls in a single day, restoring limbs, eyes and life itself, and that no other cleric living has done the like;
>
> That the High Cleric shall travel where he wills, unhindered and untaxed, and that any who would harm, hinder, seize or hold him against his will shall answer to the Crown as for treason;
>
> That those seeking the High Cleric's healing shall make their request through the **Adventurers' Guild of Millbrook**, which shall keep a proper record of the same.
>
> **Given under His Majesty's seal, at Lumenhold,** in the first year of the sealing of the wall.

"Uh..." I said. "Well, that about covers it."

I sat down at the table with Prue and told her everything that happened. It took a while, and she never said anything, patiently waiting for me to finish.

"So, Tired Guy became Eddy?" asked Prue.

"Yes."

"And you became his only cleric?" she asked.

"Yes."

"And you really did all of that healing stuff?" she asked.

"Yes. But I had to do it over a few days."

She sat there staring at me for a moment.

"Evan," she sighed, "what am I going to do with you?"

"I was kind of hoping for at least a kiss," I said.

She shook her head and laughed.

"Prue," I said, "I'm staying. Not just for a night. For as long as you'll have me."

Prue stared at me again. "Oh, Evan." She pulled me in for a kiss.
