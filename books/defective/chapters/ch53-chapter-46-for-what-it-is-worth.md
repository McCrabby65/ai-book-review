# Chapter 46: For What It Is Worth

After we were done planning, Orrin took Vagid and me to see the entrance from a safe distance. It looked like a crude mine entrance. There was an encampment of guards and mages to one side of the entrance.

*This is** where they dragged my body out and threw it away. Like trash.*

I sat down and leaned against a tree facing away from the entrance. I closed my eyes and took several deep breaths. I remembered what Tired Guy had said. *The past is set in stone. The future is like a river.*

I felt a hand on my shoulder, and when I opened my eyes, I saw Vagid and Orrin watching me with concern.

*The future is like a river. Don't fight the river.*

"Are you going to be okay?" asked Vagid.

"Yeah," I said. "It just brought back some not-fun memories."

"I'm sure," said Orrin.

"Were you able to figure out their schedules from here?" asked Vagid.

"The guards are always up at dawn. They have overlapping perimeter guards who rotate out every six hours. The mages usually don't get up until after the guards have breakfast ready. There is a head mage who keeps them on a schedule," said Orrin.

"Now we just need Johan to figure out the guard movements in the cave and chamber," said Vagid.

We headed back to camp.

"Johan, were you the one carrying the buckets of sand up the mountain?" I asked.

Johan nodded.

"Were there other buckets? I think we can get more ready for tomorrow before it gets dark," I said.

"Good idea," said Vagid. "Orrin, you join them."

"It would go faster if we both joined them," said Orrin.

"Then who would watch over Nara while she rested?" asked Vagid.

"Nara's asleep," said Orrin.

"Exactly. Very dangerous time," said Vagid. "Unless you would like to explain to Rowan and Isla how their daughter was eaten by bears when we left her alone."

I grabbed Orrin's shoulder and pulled him with me as I followed Johan. We found that Johan had borrowed the buckets from the foreman, who was more than happy to lend us a few more.

"Rory's doing a great job," said the foreman as we left. *The mouse, not the miner. Still alive and keeping Shaft Five safe.*

We were able to get around eight buckets of sand carried up the slope before it got dark. We piled it behind a stand of trees.

We headed back to camp at dusk. Vagid had used the leftovers from lunch to make a stew for dinner, and it smelled amazing.

Nara was awake and looked more rested, though still tired.

The stew was just as good as it smelled. When we finished, Orrin said, "We've had Johan join the watch rotation, so that Nara could get more rest."

"Makes sense," said Vagid. "Now that there are more of us, we should be able to drop Nara from the watch to let her sleep more."

"I can still do my watch," said Nara. "And Johan is doing magic too."

"Johan's magic doesn't wear him out like yours does. He's Otherborn," said Vagid.

"You need more sleep," said Johan.

"Fine," said Nara. "Did you get our families somewhere safe?"

"Yes. Just like we planned," said Vagid. "Nothing much happened on the trip."

"That kiss that Mara gave you before leaving seemed important," I said, smirking.

"Evan!" said Vagid.

"Were Ruta and Faren there?" asked Orrin.

"They were," I said. "The rest of the trip in their coach was priceless."

Orrin was laughing so hard he fell off his seat.

Nara walked over and gave Vagid a friendly hug. "I'm so happy for you."

"Prue kissed Evan too," said Vagid.

"Yeah, she's done that before," said Orrin. "It's more fun to harass you about it."

Later, everyone went to bed and I started my watch. The time passed quickly. I woke Johan for his turn, and then went to bed.

The next morning, we had a light breakfast of bread and cheese. Nara looked much better after a good sleep.

Vagid outlined the day for us. "Orrin, you will be on guard duty. Evan and I will be on bucket duty. Nara and Johan will be on magic duty. Nara to move the sand, and Johan to keep drilling and figure out the watch rotation in the cave and chamber."

We all nodded, then split up for our tasks. My day became monotonous. *Load sand. Carry sand. Dump sand. Repeat.*

By lunchtime, both Vagid and I were dripping with sweat. The air was cold, but we were too hot from the work to wear our coats or shirts. Our bodies were steaming with our effort.

We ate jerky at lunch so we could keep going. *Halfway. Maybe. Another five days, if Nara held up.*

By dinnertime, both Vagid and I were too tired to move. Orrin caught some large birds and cooked them on spits over the fire.

After we finished dinner, Vagid asked, "Did you find out about the watch rotation in the cave and chamber?"

"Yes, the watch in the chamber mirrors the camp," said Johan.

"That makes sense," said Vagid. "How about the drilling?"

"About a foot today," said Johan.

"Whose feet?" asked Orrin. "Mine or Vagid's?"

"A half pace today," said Johan. *He must be tired too.*

"That little?" said Orrin.

"Groundwater is not like a pool of water. It is water that is spread over a large area. It takes time to bring it together," said Johan.

"Oh. Okay," said Orrin.

"It will go faster as I drill further," said Johan.

I stretched through my whole watch so I could move in the morning. It half worked. When my watch was done, I switched with Johan.&#32;

The next day, we went through the same routine. I was sore at the start, but Vagid and I were able to find our rhythm again. When we returned to camp before dinner, we sat down on a log near the fire and waited for the others to show up. As they walked into camp, Nara made a face and put her hand over her nose.

"You two need to go wipe down before dinner," she said.

Vagid and I looked at each other and shrugged.

"Johan, take them downwind and show them where they can get cleaned up," said Nara. "Orrin, find us something for dinner."

After grabbing some clean clothes to change into, Vagid and I followed Johan, who led us to a small stream.

We took our sweaty clothes off and began wiping down.

"Evan," said Johan. "You have a lot of scars."

I sighed. "Yeah. They didn't like it when I didn't have any abilities."

"They did this to you, because you didn't have an ability?" asked Johan.

"He was a mess when we found him," said Vagid. "He was floating in the river, all torn up."

Suddenly, Orrin stepped out from behind a tree and said, "And naked."

Both Vagid and I jumped. Johan didn't.

"Much like now," said Johan. *Hey, was that a joke?*

"All the fingers on his hand were broken and poking out at odd angles," said Vagid.

I held up the hand and wiggled my fingers.

"It looked like someone tried to skin his arm," said Orrin.

I pointed to the scar from the skinning.

"He had half a dozen stab wounds," said Vagid.

I pointed to several of them.

"And lots of burn marks," said Orrin.

I pointed to a few of those.

"What are the scraped-up-looking areas from?" asked Johan.

"Clubbing," I said. "They saved the best for last."

While Vagid and I finished getting cleaned up and got dressed, Johan sat staring at the ground.

We gathered our smelly clothes and prepared to leave. Johan had not moved.

"Johan?" I asked.

"I thought it was just me," said Johan. "But they were cruel to you too."

"That's what bad people do," I said.

"Then we need to make sure they never do it again," said Johan.

"That's the plan," said Vagid.

We walked back to camp. I noticed that Johan, who normally kept his head down, was now walking with his head up.

"That took longer than I expected," said Nara when we returned. "And you found Orrin too."

"I was on my way back. I heard them, so I went to see," said Orrin. "Johan asked about Evan's scars, so we told him all the ones we knew about."

"Oh," said Nara. She looked over to me with a sad expression.

"It's okay," I said. "It's in the past now."

Orrin had caught a small boar, which he had already dressed. Vagid cooked the ribs and the flanks on spits.

Johan brought his plate to the log where Nara sat. "May I?"

Nara picked up her plate and moved to sit by Orrin.

Johan sat down alone. He kept his head up.

Everyone turned in for the night, and I stretched, then sat on the log by the fire.

*Tired Guy?* I asked.

*Yes, Evan,* came his tired voice.

*If we destroy the chamber, can you send one of us* *back?* I asked.

*I would need the power of all the gods, but it should be possible.*

*Is there any way to send both of us?* I asked.

*Not both. The wall **is too fragile for two.*

*I love her,* I said. *I don't want to lose that.*

*The decision must be yours, Evan. No one can make it for you.*

*We're almost ready for our attack on the chamber,* I said. *I just wish I was ready to make my decision.*

*You will be ready when you need to be.*

*I hope so,* I said.

*Evan, you have my blessing. For what it is worth.*

*It's worth a lot to me,* I said.

The next morning, we had a cold breakfast, then went back to our tasks.

At lunchtime, Vagid and I were eating jerky and resting when Nara and Johan came into camp.

"They're here," said Johan.

"How many?" asked Vagid.

"Many more than before." He paused. "And horses."
