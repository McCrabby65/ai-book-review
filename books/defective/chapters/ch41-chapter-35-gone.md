# Chapter 35: Gone

In the morning, I went to the café for breakfast. Nara and Vagid were just arriving. Orrin was already there with a set of empty plates.

"Already done?" I asked as I walked up.

"Halfway through, honestly," said Orrin. *How can he eat this much?*

Vagid, Nara and I ordered our meals.

"So, Vagid," I said. "Have you asked Mara out to dinner again?"

His ears flamed red. "Not yet."

"Don't wait too long," I said. "There are a lot of muscular guys who've been hanging around the Guild watching her."

*I could say** "trying to kill her,"* *but does he really need to know that? Probably not.*

Vagid looked up, alarmed. "Really?"

"Mara said she really enjoyed dinner," said Nara. "You should ask her out again."

"The sooner you get her committed to you, the better," said Orrin sagely.

"Second dates often end with a kiss," I said. "Just saying."

"I hate that we're leaving town right now," said Orrin. "My family is heading to the festival, and I'm going to miss it."

"Festival?" I asked.

"There's a harvest festival held in Garnerby every year. It's a pretty big deal," said Nara. "My family will be going too."

"Mine too," said Vagid. "Well, my mom and dad at least."

"Orrin, why are you upset about missing it?" I asked.

"There's a dance in the evening, and always lots of unmarried girls there!" said Orrin. *Of course.*

"You know your little sister will be one of those girls one day," said Vagid.

"And I'll make sure to scare any boys away, like a good brother should."

"Well, at least you'll get to see Garnerby," said Vagid. "The coach road goes right past it."

"If the coach is on time, we might make the dance!" said Orrin.

"We are not stopping for the dance," said Vagid.

After finishing breakfast, we each went back to our own places and gathered our gear for the trip. We met back at the coach yard before noon. I was surprised to see both Prue and Mara there.

"We're taking lunch," said Mara.

"Della wasn't happy," said Prue. "But we said we'd bring her some curry."

Vagid's ears were red again at the sight of Mara.

"Hi, Vagid!" said Mara.

"Mara," said Vagid. "What are you doing here?"

"She just said that," said Prue.

"Well, enjoy your lunch," said Vagid, then began loading his gear.

Prue and I looked at each other. "I heard about the harvest festival," I said. "I'm sorry I can't take you there this year."

"There'll be other years."

*I hope.*

I leaned close to her and gave her a quick kiss. Then she grabbed my collar and kissed me longer. *Apparently, my kiss isn't** up to snuff.*

"Oh, look," said Mara to Vagid. "Isn't that sweet."

Vagid couldn't figure out what to say, so he just stood there with his flaming ears.

Mara came over and gave me a hug and whispered in my ear, "Files list next of kin."

*Crap!*

*But they're out of town. Garnerby, in a crowd of hundreds. Safer there than here.*

*Probably.*

I nodded to Mara.

We each paid our fare, and a ray more for a cushion. *I might as well be comfortable.*

As the coach left, I waved to Prue and Mara.

"You and Mara seemed close," said Vagid. "Did she say anything to you?"

"She said she'll see you when we get back," I said. *I promised. Mostly.*

The coach was making good time. Orrin kept checking the sun to see if we'd make the dance. He also ate all of the food that Nara had packed before we got to the first bend. Nara spent her time sketching. Her art was amazing, and I couldn't wait to see it blossom.

I thought back to the night before. Prue pulling me in. "Try not to die again." *Probably.*

*Tired Guy?* I asked.

*Evan, how have you been?* asked a tired voice.

*A lot has happened. I told Prue that I love her.*

*I know. I was there**. You have been busy, I see.*

*Just another day in paradise,* I said with a chuckle.

*Are—*

*Tired Guy?*

Nothing. Not even hold music. *Bad. Very bad.*

"Where are we?" I asked.

"We're nearing Garnerby. Why?" asked Vagid.

"Something's wrong. See if you can get the coach to stop."

Suddenly the coach stopped. Vagid and I looked at each other, then climbed out to see what was wrong.

Vagid called up to the teamster, "Why did we stop?"

He pointed ahead. "It's gone."

"What? What's gone?" asked Vagid.

The teamster just kept pointing.

Vagid climbed up beside the teamster and looked.

"Gods above!" He jumped down. "Get your gear. Now!"

Nara jumped out of the coach first and cast Search.

She lowered her hands. "There are heartbeats," she said. "Not enough." Then she gathered her gear.

Orrin didn't say a word. He didn't even ask about the dance. Just grabbed his gear.

"Gone how?" I asked.

Vagid didn't answer.

I took my pack and followed the others toward what was left of Garnerby.

The valley was full of brown water. No rain. Not a cloud in the sky. The road ran down the hill and straight into it. A festival ribbon turned slowly in the current.

*Damn it! This is my fault!*
