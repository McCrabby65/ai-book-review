# Chapter 37: They Did

I started calling Orrin's name as I walked. I kept calling his name until he came running out of the dark.

His face was tear-streaked through the dirt. "Katia!"

"Orrin, River Guy found me," Katia said as he took her from my arms.

He ran back to the survivor camp without a word.

I followed along at a slower pace. I was dreading having to tell him it was my fault.

A few minutes later, I reached the survivor camp.

Vagid was still giving orders, and Nara was calling out locations to searchers.

I walked over to the healer's tent, where Orrin and his family were anxiously waiting on the healer to help Katia. No one noticed me.

"Orrin, I'm sorry," I said quietly. "This is all my fault."

Orrin turned around and stepped up to me. I took a step back. "What?" He was practically yelling. "You didn't flood the town. *They* did. You found her."

He stopped. I could almost see the lightbulb go on.

"Where? Where did you find her? Take us there!"

He dragged me out of the tent. I tried to keep up, but I was just too tired. He went over to Vagid.

"Vagid! Evan found Katia. We're going back to see if we can find others." Then we left the camp, heading back downstream to where I'd found Katia.

We searched until morning. I set up a fire and gathered every dry cloak we could find. Orrin directed the searchers, and when they found someone, he had them brought to me. I had two survivors helping me. We went through the same process I had taken with Katia. The first one they brought me was an old man, blue at the lips. By dawn I'd stopped counting. We were able to save everyone who was still alive.

Around midmorning, a relief group showed up. I walked them through the process of treating hypothermia, and told them to come get me if they ran into trouble.

Orrin and I walked back to the camp in silence.

I sat down at the first fire I came to. I don't remember falling asleep, but I must have. The next thing I remember is Vagid waking me up. It was late afternoon. He had some bread and cheese that we shared.

"Do you remember what I told you to do?" asked Vagid.

"You told me to go downstream and look for survivors," I replied.

"No. I told you to look for survivors," he said.

"What's the difference?"

"Evan, I didn't tell you where to look. You didn't need me to," he said.

"How is your family?" I asked.

"Mother has cracked ribs." He tore off a piece of bread. "She's telling everyone it happened because I never visit."

"Is she okay?"

"She's complaining." Something in his face eased. "So, yes."

"How about Nara's family? Have you heard anything about them?" I asked.

Vagid nodded across the camp. Pip was showing his bandaged arm to anyone who would look. I could see Nara resting with her family.

I got up and walked over to see for myself.

"Evan! Look! I got a cut! Will it scar?" Pip yelled as he saw me walking over. "Tell me it'll scar."

"Probably," I said. "Where I come from, we have a saying. Pain is temporary, glory is forever, and chicks dig scars."

Pip's eyes went wide. "Chicks?"

*Right. No chicks in that sense here.* "Girls. Girls like scars."

Nara closed her eyes. "Evan. Don't encourage him."

Pip was already repeating it under his breath like a prayer.

I walked back to where I had fallen asleep, but stopped when I saw someone familiar.

A man in plain clothes was standing across the camp. He was dirty from helping the survivors, but he stood like he was used to giving orders. *Fancy Guard? Here?*

The man was staring at me and had been for a few seconds. Then he turned and walked to a stand of trees at the edge of the camp.

*It looks like Fancy Guard, but I need to be sure.*

I crossed the camp and stepped into the trees. Nothing. No one walking away. No one behind the trunks.

"Thirteen," I heard a voice from behind me.

I froze. Nobody had called me that since the stone table.

I turned slowly to see Fancy Guard standing there, putting a bandage on his arm. *Fresh cut from a knife.*

"You should not have come. They knew you would," said Fancy Guard.

"Who knew?" I asked.

Fancy Guard looked up at me from his bandaging, then continued tying his bandage.

"They are watching the roads north. Go around," he said.

"Why would you help me?" I asked.

"The one who did this," he said, looking around, "was the one before you. Twelve."

"Why did he do this?" I asked.

"When they are finished with him, he goes north. To his drawings and models," said Fancy Guard.

I thought back to the drawings on the archive wall. The initials J. N.

*The amazing artist flooded Garnerby.*

"He did not choose this, just as you did not choose this. None of you did," he said. Then he turned and quietly left.

I stood there for a few minutes, still shocked. I needed to share this information, but Plan B might not take the news well. And I couldn't just tell them in the middle of camp.

I went back to camp and found Vagid.

"I have news we need to discuss. Privately," I said.

He looked at me, then nodded, and we gathered Nara and Orrin.

We walked downstream until we were out of sight of the camp and stopped on a gravel bank. Nara sat down hard on a rock.

"I was given some information a few minutes ago," I said. "The person who did this was the summon before me. Twelve."

"Who told you?" asked Vagid. "Tired Guy?"

I took a deep breath. "No. It was the man from the chamber. The one who read our file. He warned us."

Orrin's mouth fell open. Nara went pale. They looked at each other, then back at me. "Why?" asked Nara.

"I asked that, but he didn't say," I told them. "He wouldn't answer any of my questions, just gave me the information."

"Start at the beginning," said Vagid.

So I relayed everything that Fancy Guard had told me.

"What did he mean by 'he didn't choose this'?" asked Nara. "He destroyed Garnerby! He cut Pip's arm open! He broke Ruta's ribs! He almost killed Katia! What part did he not choose?"

"He didn't choose to be a monster," I said, more softly. "None of us did. Not Rachel, not Xavier, not me."

"Pfft! You're not a monster!" said Nara. "The others chose to not be monsters!"

"But they were still forced to do monstrous things. Kill people," I said quietly. "I would have been treated the same if I had any magic."

Nara didn't look convinced. *She looks like she's going to kill someone. I just hope it isn't me.*

"There's one more thing. I know this is hard. But until we know why he's helping us, we can't let this get out," I said.

Nara glanced at me, then looked away. *Definitely not happy.*

"If he is an ally, revealing it could put him in danger and hurt our plan," said Vagid. "We need to keep this quiet."

He looked at each of us until we nodded. Nara was last. I didn't think she would agree.

"Fine," said Nara. "I'll keep quiet about him." Then she stood. Her hands were shaking, and the air around us had gone very still.

"But we find Twelve," she said. "And he pays for every one of them."
