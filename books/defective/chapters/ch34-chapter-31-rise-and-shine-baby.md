# Chapter 31: Rise and Shine, "Baby"

Vagid woke us when his shift ended. Time was hard to judge in a cave.

"How's Orrin?" asked Nara. "Any change?"

"No change, but it looks like he's not getting any worse," said Vagid. "I'll mix another dose of the antidote. We'll need to split up. I'll go see if I can rent or buy a cart from the person who put in the job request, but Evan, you and Nara need to get Orrin out of this cave and wait for me at the entrance."

"My legs are getting a great workout," I said. "I hope Prue likes guys with muscley legs."

"Most women like men with muscles," said Nara. "As long as they don't act like being in their presence is a gift."

"Orrin! Hey, Orrin!" said Vagid.

"Hmm. Five more minutes, baby," said Orrin.

Vagid looked at Nara, who slapped Orrin's butt with her palm. "Rise and shine, 'Baby.'"

"Ouch! What was that for?" asked Orrin. "And why do I have a really foul taste in my mouth?"

"Do you feel like you can sit up?" asked Vagid.

"Maybe?" said Orrin.

Nara helped Vagid roll Orrin over on his uninjured side, then sit him up. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" said Orrin. "I feel weak as a kitten for some reason. What happened?"

"What is the last thing you remember?" asked Vagid.

"We were in the archives. I was thinking you were all taking your time, then... nothing," said Orrin.

"An assassin cut your back with a poisoned blade. I don't think he was trying to kill you immediately, but it's made you very sick," said Vagid.

"Well, that explains things," said Orrin.

"How are you feeling?" asked Nara.

"Tired, and weak, mainly," said Orrin. "Oh, and hungry."

"You might want to hold off on the food until after you take the antidote," suggested Vagid.

Vagid handed Orrin a mug. "Drink this."

Orrin took a small sip. "Ugh, it tastes horrible. No thanks."

"Well, I guess we can bury him outside the cave," said Nara.

"Why bother? We don't have shovels, and the monsters will just eat him," I said. "We didn't bury the assassin."

"True," said Vagid. "It wouldn't seem fair to bury one but not the other."

"Okay! I'll drink it," said Orrin.

Orrin emptied the mug, then looked like he might throw up, but was able to keep it down.

"Let that settle, then we can see if you're still hungry," said Nara.

Vagid helped Nara tie Orrin to my back, then picked up his pack and left. Nara took the rest of the packs. Nara, Orrin and I slowly made our way back out of the cave. We stopped often, since my legs were still tired from the stairs. I wasn't sure how long it took us to reach the entrance to the cave, but it looked past noon when we made it. Nara cast Search before we exited the cave. She didn't find anything.

"Orrin, how're you doing?" I asked.

"You're not a very good pack animal, too bony," he said.

"We are so even now," I said.

"I'm still not naked, so not even," said Orrin.

I heard Nara sigh.

"Do you feel any stronger?" asked Nara.

"Not really," said Orrin. "And I hate to bring it up, but I need to pee."

I turned to Nara. She looked at me.

"No," she said.

"Okay, but at least help me untie him from my back," I said.

"That I can do," she said.

I walked over to a tree. We untied him and I gently lowered his feet to the ground. He leaned against the tree and peed. Nara and I studied the treetops.

After Orrin finished, we talked about what we needed to do next.

"Should we try to make progress toward Sedgewick, or the road?" I asked.

"No. Vagid knows where we are. If we head off, there is more chance that we'll miss each other," said Nara.

We didn't have long to wait. Shortly after Orrin finished his bathroom break, we saw Vagid pulling a small cart toward the cave.

"You got back quick," I said.

"I was able to buy a cart. It's nothing fancy, but it should work to get Orrin back to Sedgewick so we can board the coach," said Vagid.

We put our packs in the cart like pillows and then put Orrin on them. "Since I'm the least experienced at fighting, I'll pull the cart," I said.

"I'll be out front. Nara, can you clear our tracks as we go?" asked Vagid.

"Of course," said Nara.

We started on our way back to Sedgewick. Vagid stayed in front of me and every few minutes I could hear Nara cast "Clean Sweep," which blew the cart tracks away.

"Were you able to get the job slip taken care of while you were in Sedgewick?" asked Nara while we were walking.

"Yeah. We should be good to just get on the coach once we get back to Sedgewick," said Vagid.

The cart made the trip much easier, though my legs were still wobbly by the time we stopped. Orrin reviewed my cart-pulling the whole way, which was easy for a man lying on a pillow of packs. Vagid had us stop in the woods before we got to Sedgewick. We didn't want everyone to remember an injured man in a cart later. We set up our camp. I gathered wood and started a fire, and Vagid made a stew from the jerky and cheese again. Orrin ate some, but said his stomach was not feeling good, so he quit after a few spoons. I took first watch. Every time Orrin coughed, Vagid was awake and checking his breathing before I could turn around. Nobody said what we were all thinking: someone had sent that assassin, and he hadn't been after goblin ears.

The next morning, Orrin was feeling stronger, but not enough to walk all the way back to the Sedgewick coach stop. We decided to pull the cart as close to Sedgewick as we could without being seen. We left the cart by a stand of trees. Hopefully, someone would be able to use it.

Orrin leaned on me while we walked into Sedgewick. It wasn't very far to the coach stop, and once we got there, Orrin was able to sit down and rest.

Just before noon, the coach pulled up. We loaded the gear on and helped Orrin climb in. At dusk, the coach stopped at an inn and we had a simple dinner, then went to our room.

The next morning, we got back on the coach and continued to Millbrook. By dinnertime we could finally see the earthen wall around Millbrook. The coach stopped and let us off. We took our gear and headed to the chapel.

"Nara, can you go ahead and see if Halden can meet us on the way?" asked Vagid.

"Sure," said Nara as she left.

Vagid carried Orrin's pack while I helped Orrin walk down the street. We stopped frequently to let Orrin catch his breath.

After around ten minutes Nara and Halden came back. Halden looked at Orrin and said, "Rocks?"

"Yeah... sort of," said Orrin.

"Mid Heal," said Halden. His hand glowed for several seconds. When the light went away, I could see that Orrin was standing straighter, though he still looked like he would need several days to recover completely. Halden took over my position of helping Orrin walk to the chapel. "I'll add it to his tab," he said.

Once Halden had Orrin in a room, he cast another spell, "Purify."&#32;

"What does that do?" I asked.

"It removes any toxins in your body," replied Halden. "Orrin will need to stay here for a few days."

"Will there be any lasting damage?" I asked.

"No. He'll have a scar, but he'll be his normal self," said Halden.

Vagid and Nara left for their own homes, and I walked to the river where Plan B had carved the boulder to talk to Tired Guy.

*Tired Guy, we made it back and we found more information. Orrin got hurt, but we were able to get him to Halden for healing, so he should be fine in a few days.*

*You came out,* said a tired voice. *I'm glad. I'm sorry to hear about Orrin. Halden is an excellent healer. Orrin is in good hands.*

*We found something odd in the archives. There were drawings of buildings from my world. They look familiar to me, but I don't remember where I saw them. The artist left their initials, J. N. Is there a summoned with those initials?*

*I have only spoken with the ones who came to me after they died, Evan. None of the ones who have come to me had the initials J. N.*

*So whoever drew them is still alive?*

*Or died in a place where I cannot see.*

*Does the phrase "the keep on the grey water" mean anything to you?*

*I have not heard of a place like that.*

*You heard me tell Prue about you. I hope you don't mind.*

*I do not mind. As with you, it is up to her whether she believes.*

*Good night.*

*Good night, Evan.*

I walked back to the chapel and went to bed.

The next morning, after Stella's class, I checked in on Orrin before heading to the café for breakfast. Orrin asked that I bring a fully cooked orc. He also denied ever saying "Not the face." I refused and said I would bring him back something simple. He wasn't happy about that, but unless he wanted to eat Halden's cooking, he would have to make do with what he got.

I met Vagid and Nara at the café. I told them what Orrin had asked me to bring back.

Vagid's hand kept drifting to his sword hilt while we ate. I don't think he noticed.

"It's not like he can eat a whole orc," said Nara. "He's just being dramatic."

"Sounds like he's feeling better though," said Vagid.

"Before we go see Orrin, let's drop by the Guild. We can turn in the job and Vagid can ask Mara about 'the keep on the grey water,' whatever that is," I said.

"You could just ask Prue, couldn't you?" said Vagid.

"Yes, but I will already be asking Prue to dinner, and that will be three dinners to your zero dinners," I said. "So you need to get started."

Vagid's face was bright red.

We left for the Guild right after breakfast. Once we got to the Guild, Vagid joined Mara's line and I joined Prue's line. Nara decided to wait by the door rather than be involved in our dating shenanigans.

*Probably the wisest decision.*

"Hi, Vagid! It's so good to see you again!" said Mara when Vagid got to the front of the line.

"Hi, Mara. We finished the Sedgewick job," said Vagid as he handed the bag of goblin ears over along with the signed job slip. "And I was wondering if you had some information."

"Information about what?" asked Mara.

"He wants you to go out with him on a date," I said loudly, before Vagid could answer.

"Oof!" I said as Vagid punched me in the gut. He didn't even turn around to do it.

*So much for not deciding for other people.*

"Actually, I was going to ask if you had any information about a place called 'the keep on the grey water'?" said Vagid.

"So you don't want to go on a date with me?" said Mara.

"No! I mean, yes, I do, but that wasn't the question I was going to ask you," said Vagid.

Mara looked confused. "I'm not sure I understand. You do want to go on a date or you don't?"

"I do want to go on a date with you," said Vagid.

"Okay! I love dinner! Pick me up when the Guild closes. The curry place!" said Mara.

"Um. Great!" said Vagid as he turned to leave.

"You forgot the information you asked about," said Mara, holding out a slip to Vagid. I didn't see her look anything up.

Vagid took the slip and wandered back over to where Nara was standing.

"Pushy as always," said Prue with a smirk.

"You never make any of the shots you don't take," I said. "I worked up the courage to ask you out, so I figured Vagid just needed a little help."

"Courage? Asking me out to dinner doesn't seem very courageous," said Prue.

Prue raised one eyebrow and waited.

"You're very intimidating," I said.

"So why are you bothering me today?" asked Prue.

"I came by to ask you to dinner," I said, smiling.

"Hm. We can go tonight, but you have to choose where. I've chosen both of the other times," said Prue. She lowered her voice. "Your soldiers come back tomorrow."

"Done," I said. Then, quieter: "I know. Thank you." I'd be here at closing.

I rejoined Nara and Vagid by the door. Vagid was looking a little scared.

"That wasn't so hard, was it?" I asked.

"What am I going to do?" said Vagid. "I don't know what to say on a date."

"Ask the cook where the curry came from," I said. "Instant conversation."

*And maybe I'll finally get an answer.*

I looked at Nara. Nara looked back at me. I sighed and said, "Nara, can you take Vagid to the same shop you showed me and help him buy a souvenir to give Mara at the end of the date?"

I reached over and took the slip from Vagid's hand. He had other things to worry about.

The slip said Greywater Keep. Maybe whoever minded it could tell me who J. N. was.
