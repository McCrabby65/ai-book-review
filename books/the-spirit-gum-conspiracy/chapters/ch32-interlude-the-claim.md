# Interlude: The Claim

**GUILD OF ARCANE CONVEYANCE** **Claim for Performance Bonus (Second)**

**Claimant:** Durgan Pell, High Ritualist of the Seventh Circle. *(License: reinstated, pending. See attached.)*

**Services rendered:**

1. Successful summoning of a Prince of the First Line. *(Prince since confirmed human. This was always the intent. See Section Fourteen, Subsection C: Unforeseen Benefits.)*
2. Successful conclusion of a war.
3. Peace. *(Personally.)*

**Expenses:**

- One goat. *(Asset. Has since consumed one royal ear in the presence of the assembled Houses, and is therefore now classified as a royal asset. Value adjusted accordingly.)*
- Rain. *(Ritually approved. Foreseen.)*
- Three days' travel at speed. *(On a wagon. With the goat.)*
- Hazard pay: struck by the will of the forest (posterior). *(Carried forward from previous claim. Still painful.)*
- Hazard pay: discovered asleep in luggage by the Inquisition. *(Emotional damages.)*

**Supporting documentation:** Form 12-B (Acknowledgment of Satisfactory Summoning; non-refundable). Form 22-D (Non-Disclosure Agreement, Domestic Fowl). Form 31-A (Archive Access, Retroactive). Form 40-C (Schedule of Remittance). Form 51-R (Certificate of Sacred Rash), forty-one copies. Two crowns from the Inquisition. *(Framed. Not for remittance.)*

**Witnesses:** Four thousand.

**Notes:** The claimant wishes to state for the record that he never once doubted the Prince. Not at the summoning. Not at the archery trial. Not in the barrel. Not even when the ear came off.

**Amount claimed:** Sufficient to settle all outstanding obligations in Under-Karrak, and to retain one forge, deep levels, eleven generations, bellows included. *And a little extra, for wine.*

**Status:** *UNDER REVIEW.*
